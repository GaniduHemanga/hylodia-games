About Hylodia Games

Built by one gamer,
for every gamer.

Hylodia Games started as a simple idea: put the tools gamers actually use every day in one fast, free place — no clutter, no unnecessary sign-ups, nothing slowing you down.

OUR MISSION

Give gamers fast, reliable tools without the noise. Every tool on Hylodia is built to load instantly, work without an account, and get out of your way — so you can get back to playing.

How it started.

A small project with a simple goal, built one tool at a time.

Hylodia Games is a solo project — one person handling the design, the build and every tool on the site. It started in 2026 out of a simple frustration: too many gaming tools were scattered across cluttered, ad-heavy sites that made simple things feel complicated.

Hylodia brings those tools together in one clean place, and keeps growing one feature at a time — based on what gamers actually ask for, not what looks good on a roadmap slide.

Why gamers stick around.

A few principles every tool on Hylodia is held to.

Fast by default

Every tool loads instantly and runs right in your browser — no downloads, no waiting around.

No account needed

Every tool works the moment you open it. Signing up is optional, never a requirement.

Privacy-first

Tools like the CPS tester and reaction time tester run fully on your device — your results aren't tracked or sold.

Built from feedback

New tools and fixes are shaped directly by what players ask for through the contact page.
