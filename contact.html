Before you send that message.

A few quick answers that might save you a trip through the form.

Does my message actually reach someone?

Yes — every submission is saved straight to Hylodia's database the moment you hit send, and it's read by an actual person, not a bot.

Do I need an account to contact you?

No. The contact form works for anyone — signing in only matters for things like saving tool settings, not for reaching out.

What should I include in a bug report?

The tool you were using, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots help but aren't required.

Can I suggest a tool that doesn't exist yet?

Definitely — pick "Tool idea" as the topic. A good chunk of what's on Hylodia today started as a message just like that.
