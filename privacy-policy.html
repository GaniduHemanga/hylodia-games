Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Hylodia Games, we prioritize your privacy. We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide our gaming utilities:

  • Account Information: Email address and encrypted authentication details provided during login or signup.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous technical parameters such as tool interactions, benchmark outputs (CPS, FPS), and error logs.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is strictly utilized to enhance platform stability, optimize tool response times, and provide seamless user account management across all Hylodia gaming services.

3. Data Protection

We employ modern industry standards including end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption and cloud infrastructure backed by Supabase. Your personal credentials are never exposed or sold to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Our authentication and analytics infrastructure utilizes secure third-party integration providers like Google OAuth and Supabase Auth strictly in accordance with privacy laws.
