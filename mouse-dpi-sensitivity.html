User Guide

How to Use This Utility

Follow these simple steps to measure your mouse DPI and lock your eDPI accurately.

1

Move Your Mouse in the DPI Box

Move your mouse inside the dotted box on the right. Your real-time speed and estimated DPI will display dynamically.

2

Click to Lock & Stop Tester

Click your left mouse button inside the box when testing. This stops the test, locks the final DPI, and automatically fills the eDPI Calculator.

3

Set In-Game Sensitivity & Copy

Enter your game sensitivity, pick your game profile, and click Copy Result to save your final configuration.
